Skardu & Shangrila Expedition

Majestic Cold Desert & Alpine Lakes

Nestled high in the Karakoram range, Skardu is the gateway to some of the highest peaks in the world, including K2. Known for its surreal contrast of sand dunes surrounded by snow-capped mountains, turquoise lakes, and historic forts, Skardu offers an unforgettable alpine adventure.

  • Shangrila Resort & Lower Kachura Lake: Heart-shaped lake surrounded by serene resorts.
  • Katpana Cold Desert: High-altitude desert dunes covered with snow in winter.
  • Deosai National Park: "Land of the Giants," the second-highest plateau in the world.
  • Satpara Lake & Shigar Fort: Crystal clear glacier water lakes and 17th-century heritage.

Naran & Kaghan Valley Escape

Crystal Clear Lakes & Mountain Pass Views

Located in the Mansehra District of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, Naran & Kaghan Valleys are among Pakistan’s most celebrated natural retreats. Featuring dense pine forests, roaring river waters, and glacial lakes, Naran is ideal for couples, families, and nature enthusiasts.

  • Lake Saif-ul-Malook: Legendary high-altitude lake reflecting Malika Parbat peak.
  • Babusar Top: Panoramic mountain pass sitting at 13,691 ft connecting Kaghan to Gilgit-Baltistan.
  • Lulusar Lake & Lalazar: Vibrant alpine lake and meadows with wildflower displays.
  • Siri Paye Meadows: A serene grassland plateau above Shogran.

Swat & Kalam Retreat

Lush Pine Forests & Cascading Waterfalls

Often called the "Switzerland of the East," Swat Valley boasts rich Buddhist history, lush green river meadows, dense pine woodlands, and snow-capped mountain peaks. Kalam Valley serves as the crown jewel of northern Swat, offering cool mountain breezes and crystal streams.

  • Malam Jabba: Pakistan’s premier ski resort area featuring zip-lining and chairlifts.
  • Kalam Valley & Ushu Forest: Ancient forest trees, scenic river banks, and mountain hospitality.
  • Mahodand & Kundol Lakes: Glacial lakes accessible via 4x4 jeep trails.
  • Fizagat Park & Swat Museum: Local cultural centers and riverside relaxing spots.
